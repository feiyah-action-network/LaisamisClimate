Programs · The Work

Four programs.
One movement for the land.

Everything LCLI does serves one goal: the communities of Laisamis holding legal title to their land and thriving on it as the climate changes. Each program is community-mandated, NLC-anchored, and open to funders who want their support to reach the ground.

What We Do — And What It Needs

The programs.
And how to back them.

Program 01

Community Land Registration

The flagship: legal title for 1.85 million hectares

Only 64 of Kenya's ~1,000 community land units hold title. We drive registration for the five communities of Laisamis under the Community Land Act — with trained local paralegals doing the work, not outside consultants.

  • Train Laisamis youth as community land paralegals
  • Community sensitisation and CLMC formation across all six wards
  • Registration applications, documentation, and follow-through with the NLC
  • Documented community consent at every step
Seeking: multi-year program grants · Land tenure & legal empowerment funders
Program 02

Land Rights Advocacy & Campaigns

Northern Kenya's voice in the national registration push

We anchor the Sajili Ardhi ya Jamii registration campaign in Marsabit and carry pastoralist land rights into the International Year of Rangelands and Pastoralism — from ward assemblies to national policy rooms.

  • Community assemblies and civic education on the Community Land Act
  • Coalition work with land rights and pastoralist organisations
  • Advocacy for faster, cheaper registration and NLC resourcing
  • Early-warning response when community land is threatened
Seeking: campaign & movement funding · Climate justice funds
Program 03

Climate Resilience

Adaptation owned by the communities on the frontline

Community solar, water harvesting, and rangeland restoration across four ecological zones — built with local hands, governed by community structures, and designed so revenue and power stay in Laisamis.

  • Community-owned solar for homes, schools, clinics, and boreholes
  • Water harvesting and storage for drought resilience
  • Rangeland restoration led by herders' own knowledge
  • Youth trained as operators and technicians
Seeking: resilience & energy-access funding · Community energy programs
Program 04

Carbon & Land Accountability

The watchdog: no deal about us without us

Kenya's 2024 carbon regulations make consent and a 40% community benefit share mandatory for land-based projects. We make sure northern Kenya's communities know it — and that the market knows we are watching.

  • Monitor carbon projects and land deals touching community land
  • Community rights education on the carbon regulations and FPIC
  • Published field intelligence and public reporting
  • Ground-truth data collection by community monitors for researchers (earned income that funds the watch)
Seeking: environmental defender funds · Media & research partnerships
How We Work

Community mandate first.
Then the work.

1

The community decides

Priorities come from ward assemblies and the community structures, under NLC oversight — not from funders, and not from us. Consent is documented before anything begins.

2

Programs get designed and funded

We turn community mandates into fundable programs with real budgets, benefit-sharing, and reporting funders can stand behind.

3

Local people deliver

Paralegals, monitors, and technicians from Laisamis run the work. Skills, jobs, and authority stay in the community.

Legal clarity: approximately 173,700 hectares are subject to an active High Court case and are excluded from all programs until resolved. All work proceeds only on undisputed land and under the community governance structures described on our Governance page.
