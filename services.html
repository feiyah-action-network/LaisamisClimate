Structured access to Africa's
most credible climate testbed.

Four ways to work with LCLI — each built on documented community consent, NLC-anchored governance, and a trained local field team across 1.85 million hectares and four ecological zones.

What You Can Engage Us For

Four offerings.
One access point.

Service 01

Site Scoping & Zone Matching

For climate technology companies planning ASAL deployment

A structured feasibility engagement that matches your technology to the right ecological conditions before you commit to a pilot.

  • Zone-matching across highland forest, semi-arid slopes, arid grassland, and desert
  • Community introductions through the ward CLMC structures
  • Site logistics, access, and workforce assessment
  • Regulatory groundwork map (EPRA, NEMA, county permits)
Fixed-fee engagement · Written scoping report
Service 02

Pilot Hosting & FPIC Facilitation

For funded pilots ready to deploy

End-to-end hosting of technology pilots on community land, with consent documented to the standard Kenya's carbon and land regulations now demand.

  • Documented free, prior and informed consent through community assemblies
  • Trained local field monitors and technicians
  • Baseline environmental data for your deployment site
  • Ongoing community liaison for the life of the pilot
Setup fee + monthly hosting retainer
Service 03

Field Data & Monitoring Contracts

For MRV providers, carbon developers, and researchers

Recurring ground-truth data collection by trained community monitors — the field measurements that satellite-based carbon and climate models depend on.

  • Geotagged vegetation, soil, and rangeland condition measurements
  • Seasonal monitoring across four ecological zones
  • Sampling support for soil carbon and biomass studies
  • Local logistics for field campaigns and site visits
Per-campaign or annual contract
Service 04

Carbon Origination & Consent Advisory

For carbon project developers and buyers

Kenya's 2024 carbon regulations make community consent and benefit-sharing mandatory. We build the consent architecture that keeps projects on the market.

  • FPIC processes compliant with the Climate Change (Carbon Markets) Regulations 2024
  • Community development agreement facilitation
  • NLC-anchored governance design for land-based projects
  • Buyer matching on commission — no upfront cost to communities
Advisory fee + success-based commission
How Engagement Works

Consent first.
Then deployment.

1

Scoping conversation

Tell us what you want to deploy, measure, or originate. We respond within 48 hours with a zone match and a proposed engagement structure.

2

Community consent

Every engagement goes through the community structures — documented FPIC via ward assemblies under NLC oversight. No agreement is signed before consent is.

3

Deployment with a local team

Trained community monitors and technicians run the fieldwork. Revenue flows through agreed benefit-sharing from day one.

Legal clarity: approximately 173,700 hectares are subject to an active High Court case and are excluded from all engagements until resolved. All agreements use only undisputed, NLC-registered land, and land-based carbon engagements comply with Kenya's mandatory community benefit-sharing requirements.
